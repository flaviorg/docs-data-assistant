# Privacy and data protection

## What data we collect

To process orders, we collect your name, taxpayer ID (CPF), delivery address, email and phone number. When you pay by card, the card details go straight to the payment processor and do not stay with us. We also record your purchase history and, with your consent, your coffee preferences to personalize recommendations. Processing follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD).

## How we use the data

We use the data to deliver orders, issue invoices, provide support, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, send news by email. We do not sell personal data. We share only what is necessary with carriers, payment processors and the cloud provider, all under confidentiality agreements. Internal sales reports use data aggregated by city, state and segment, without identifying customers.

## Automated support

The website's automated assistant answers questions about policies and looks up aggregated sales figures, but it does not expose customers' personal data: it does not give out the names, emails, phone numbers, addresses or purchase history of anyone. To handle a specific order, human support confirms your identity before showing any information.

## Retention and deletion

Order data is kept for 5 years, as required by tax law. Data used for marketing is deleted within 30 days after you withdraw consent. Accounts with no purchases in more than 3 years are anonymized.

## Your rights and the data protection officer

You can request access to, correction, portability or deletion of your data, and withdraw consent at any time. The data protection officer (DPO) can be reached by email at privacy@lunarmill.example and replies within 15 days. If you are not satisfied with the reply, you can contact the National Data Protection Authority.
