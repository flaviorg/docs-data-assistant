# Returns and exchanges policy

This policy applies to purchases made on the website and in the app. Purchases on marketplaces also follow the rules of the platform where the order was placed.

## Right to cancel

For online purchases, you can cancel the order within 7 calendar days of receiving it, without having to give a reason. The product must come back in its original packaging and with no signs of use. For coffee and capsules, the packaging must be sealed, because we do not resell opened food. Return shipping for a cancellation is on us, and the refund includes the shipping paid on the purchase.

## Defective products

If the product arrives defective or has a manufacturing problem, the deadline to request an exchange or a return is:

- 30 days for consumables: whole beans, ground coffee, capsules and filters (broken packaging, coffee that smells of mold, a leaking capsule);
- 90 days for equipment: grinders, presses, coffee makers, scales, kettles and espresso machines.

The deadlines count from delivery. You choose between an exchange for the same item, another product of the same value or a refund. After the 90 days, a defective grinder or other piece of equipment can still be covered by the 12-month warranty, described in the equipment warranty document.

## How to request an exchange or a return

1. Go to "My orders" on the website or in the app and choose "Request an exchange or return".
2. Give the reason and, for a defect, send photos or a short video of the problem.
3. Within 2 business days, you receive a prepaid return shipping code by email.
4. When the product reaches our distribution center, the inspection takes up to 5 business days.

If you prefer, write to returns@lunarmill.example with the order number. The refund follows the refund times for each payment method.

## What we do not accept

We do not accept returns of coffee ground to order, with a custom grind, after the packaging has been opened, or of products with a broken seal and no defect. Equipment damaged by a fall, connection to the wrong voltage or cleaning with abrasive products does not count as a manufacturing defect. Limited-edition items can be exchanged only in case of a defect.
